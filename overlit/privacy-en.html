OVERLIT Privacy Policy

HyuniQ ("we") provides the mobile game OVERLIT (the "Game") and processes personal information as described below.

1. Information we process

The Game has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, phone number or email address. While you play, the following is processed:

Category Items How
Game account Anonymous player ID (a random identifier created by the Game) Created automatically on first launch
Google Play Games link Google Play Games player ID (used only to link your game account) Automatically if you are signed in to Google Play Games on the device, or Settings > Google Play Games > Link
Game data Stars per stage, in-game currency (prisms) and hint tickets, owned characters and blocks, daily check-in record, hint usage, coupons used, items given by us Saved automatically while you play
Purchase records Product ID, purchase time, purchase verification data (a hashed purchase token), store When you make an in-app purchase
Advertising Advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, device information, ad views and clicks Collected by the ad SDK (Google AdMob) when an ad is shown
Error reports Crash and error logs, device model, OS and app version, an installation ID (a random ID created for each installation) Sent automatically when the Game crashes or an error occurs
Support Your email address, your message and, if you provide it, your player ID When you email us

Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Google Play; we never receive them. The Game does not use or store your Google Play Games nickname or profile picture.

2. Why we process it

  • Saving your progress and items and keeping them available (with Google Play Games linked, also after reinstalling or on a new phone)
  • Verifying in-app purchases, preventing duplicate or fraudulent purchases, handling refunds
  • Giving daily check-in, stage and coupon rewards, and compensation after support requests
  • Showing rewarded ads you choose to watch, measuring ads and preventing ad fraud
  • Finding the causes of crashes and errors and improving stability
  • Answering your questions

3. How long we keep it

  • Game data and your game account (including the Google Play Games link): until you delete it in the Game, ask us to delete it, or the service ends.
  • Purchase records: 5 years, as required by Korean e-commerce law, then deleted.
  • Support emails: 1 year after the request is resolved.
  • Advertising data: kept and processed by Google under Google's policies.
  • Error reports: kept for Unity's service retention period, then deleted.

4. Service providers and international transfers

Recipient Country Data Purpose

Unity Technologies (Unity Diagnostics) United States and other countries where Unity's cloud servers are located Error reports (section 1) Analyzing crashes and errors
Google LLC (Google Play billing) United States Purchase information Processing in-app purchases

Data is sent over the network while you use the Game. If you do not want this, stop using the Game and request deletion (section 7).

5. Ads and your advertising ID

  • When you choose to unlock hints with an ad, the Game shows a rewarded ad provided by Google AdMob.
  • Where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent screen appears on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Google > Ads).
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties other than the service providers in section 4, unless required by law.

7. Your rights and data deletion

  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing.
  • Delete in the Game: tap Settings > Data > Delete my data. Your game data on our server, your game account (including the Google Play Games link) and the data on your device are deleted right away. This can't be undone, including purchased items.
  • Request by email: open Settings > Support > Player ID > Copy and email it to hyuniqlab@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We delete your data without delay (within 10 days at the latest) and let you know.
  • Either way, purchase records that the law requires us to keep are stored separately for the period in section 3 and then deleted.
  • Uninstalling the Game does not delete your game data on our server. Use "Delete my data" or keep your player ID before uninstalling. If you linked Google Play Games, reinstalling connects you to the same data, so you can delete it then.
  • Information in your Google Play Games profile itself is managed in your Google Account's Play Games settings.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted (TLS). Currency and purchase records can only be changed by our server, and purchases are credited only after the store's signature is verified. Only operator accounts registered on the server can give items, and every grant is logged. Access is limited to the business owner, and admin accounts use 2-step verification.

9. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (the age of consent in Korea). If we learn that we have, we delete it without delay. Parents or guardians can request deletion as described in section 7.
